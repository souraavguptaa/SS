# মা আসবে — *Maa Ashbe: The Homecoming*

An original two-minute animated short imagined for SastaSundar's Agomoni song
*Maa Ashbe | সুর থেকে সুস্থতা*. Agomoni songs welcome Uma (Durga) home from
Kailash to her mother in Bengal every autumn. This story keeps that heart and
adds the song's own idea, *sur theke sushthota*: melody that turns into wellness.

Look: a 90s hand-inked cartoon (cel shading, bold ink lines, painted skies) shot
like an 80s space-opera epic (anamorphic 2.39:1 frame, starfields, lens flares,
film grain, sweeping camera moves). Everything is built and animated in Three.js.

## Characters

| Character | Who they are | Design |
|---|---|---|
| **Uma** | The daughter-goddess, waiting on Kailash | Red saree with a gold border, gold crown, long black hair, third eye that glows when she hears the call |
| **Singho** | Her lion, a big friendly cat | Golden coat, flame-shaped mane, tufted tail |
| **Ma** | Uma's mother in a riverside Bengal home | White saree with a red border, grey bun, round spectacles, a shawl; tired and unwell at the start |
| **The Dhaki** | The drummer who wakes the town | Orange kurta, white dhoti, red-checked gamchha, a dhak drum crowned with kash plumes |

## The story in twelve beats

1. **The Cosmos.** Deep space. A slow push through stars and a violet nebula.
   A golden comet streaks past. The title *মা আসবে* gathers out of starlight.
2. **Kailash.** We drop through the clouds onto a moonlit Himalaya. Under green
   aurora, Uma sits on a ledge of the holy mountain, gazing south toward Bengal.
   Singho sleeps beside her.
3. **Ma's House.** Before dawn, by a river lined with palms. A single lit window.
   Inside, Ma pulls her shawl tight and tunes her old valve radio, the one
   that plays at dawn every year as the Puja begins. She lights a clay lamp and looks north.
4. **The Melody Rises.** Golden notes pour out of the radio, through the window
   and up into the sky: a ribbon of light (the *sur*) that winds across clouds
   and mountains toward Kailash.
5. **The Call.** The ribbon reaches Uma and circles her. Her eyes open, her
   third eye blazes, she smiles. Singho wakes with a roar that blows the snow off
   the peak.
6. **The Leap.** Uma mounts Singho and they leap off the mountain. An epic
   chase through canyons of sunrise clouds: speed lines, light streaks,
   the sun bursting over the horizon behind them.
7. **Bengal Awakens.** They sweep low over the plains. In their wake the kash
   fields burst into white bloom in a racing wave; rivers glitter; lamps in
   every village blink on one after another.
8. **Dhak!** In the town, the Dhaki strikes the first beat. Fairy lights cascade
   down the street in time with the drum, the pandal blazes gold, and people pour out.
9. **The Door.** Ma hears the drums. She rises and opens the door, and golden
   light floods in. Uma stands on the doorstep, with Singho peeking over her shoulder.
10. **Wellness.** Mother and daughter embrace. Shiuli flowers rain down. The
    golden light sinks into Ma: her back straightens, her colour returns, and she
    laughs. The melody has become *sushthota*.
11. **The Blessing.** The camera rises over the glowing town until it looks
    like a constellation. In the sky the ten-armed Durga forms out of the stars,
    with her third eye gleaming, and blesses the town below.
12. **Title.** *মা আসবে* · *সুর থেকে সুস্থতা* · *শুভ শারদীয়া*.

## Colour script

Cosmos indigo and gold → moonlit Kailash cyan with Uma's red → night-blue
house with an orange lamp → pre-dawn gold ribbon on rose sky → sunrise magenta and
amber clouds → bright kash-white morning → violet dusk town with warm
string lights → golden embrace → starry finale with a gold-and-red goddess.

## Music sync

Shot boundaries sit on musical phrase boundaries of the chosen two-minute
window, and drum hits drive light pulses, cuts and camera bumps (see
`src/timeline.js` and `audio/beats.json`).
